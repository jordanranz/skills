# Private career context model

Store only what the user authorizes. Keep this data outside the public skill package.

## Candidate

- verified experience and skills
- role levels and career direction
- location and work-model preferences
- compensation and equity preferences
- factual and confidentiality boundaries
- writing-style preferences

## Assets

- base resume
- tailored resume variants and rationale
- portfolio and profile links
- reusable stories with evidence status

## Pipeline

- company, role, link, location, and team
- status and status date
- resume and responses used
- referral or contact
- deadlines, limits, and follow-up date
- decision rationale and notes

## Policies

- candidate-AI guidance
- application-frequency limits
- submission-confirmation requirements

## Next actions

- action
- priority
- deadline
- blocking information
