# Role-fit rubric

Score each success factor with evidence, not intuition.

- `Direct`: repeated professional ownership in substantially similar work.
- `Adjacent`: professional evidence transfers with a manageable domain or scope gap.
- `Supporting`: personal projects, partial exposure, or relevant conceptual experience.
- `Missing`: no verified evidence.
- `Unknown`: evidence may exist but has not been established.

Assess constraints separately:

- role level and expected scope
- location and work model
- compensation and equity
- product or mission interest
- career direction
- application limits and timing
- referral or network advantage

Never combine these into a pseudo-precise percentage unless the user supplies a scoring model.
