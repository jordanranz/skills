# Refine Application Response

Preserve the candidate's voice and authorship while making the response specific, credible, and concise.

## Build the response

1. Read the exact prompt, character or word guidance, job posting, and employer candidate-AI policy.
2. Determine the permitted mode:
   - `Refine only`: the candidate must supply the initial draft.
   - `Interview then draft`: gather original candidate ideas before drafting.
   - `Draft from established context`: use only when policy permits and sufficient candidate-provided material already exists.
3. Gather missing content one focused question at a time. Ask for motivation, concrete evidence, personal contribution, observed outcome, and role connection only as needed.
4. Identify a single thesis. Select two or three supporting details rather than listing the resume.
5. Draft in the candidate's natural vocabulary. Prefer specific nouns and ownership verbs over generic enthusiasm.
6. Critique for unsupported claims, repetition, copied company language, excessive jargon, and AI-sounding symmetry.
7. Match the requested length and structure. Preserve the candidate's punctuation and style preferences.
8. Return a reusable final block. Do not submit without immediate confirmation.

## Response patterns

- `Why Company`: personal conviction + demonstrated attention + role-specific contribution.
- `Additional Information`: professional evidence first + distinctive supporting project or perspective.
- `Impact`: problem + specific contribution + why it mattered + evidence of success.
- `Direct-use question`: what was built + exact personal contribution + real workflow or outcome.
