# Application response integrity check

- Follow the employer's current candidate-AI policy.
- Preserve candidate authorship when an initial draft is required.
- Support every factual statement with candidate-provided context.
- Distinguish direct work, collaboration, exploration, and personal projects.
- Avoid invented impact, metrics, customer reactions, or production claims.
- Remove language that could apply to any company.
- Connect interest to the actual role rather than the employer generally.
- Keep the answer meaningfully different from responses submitted to adjacent roles.
- Confirm immediately before any application submission.
