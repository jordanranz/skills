# Resume delivery checklist

## Content

- Match the headline to the role family without overstating level or specialty.
- Establish relevant experience and scope in the opening summary.
- Support every material claim with candidate evidence.
- Keep professional and personal work distinct.
- Put recent, high-signal evidence before supporting projects.
- Use keywords naturally rather than copying the posting.
- Check titles, dates, employers, technologies, and metrics for consistency.

## Layout and delivery

- Preserve the intended page budget.
- Render and inspect every page.
- Check clipping, overlap, missing glyphs, stranded headings, broken links, and accidental gaps.
- Confirm the PDF and DOCX contain the same current content.
- Keep the original resume untouched.
- Identify the upload file and editable source clearly.
